Welcome to My Blog

2024-01-01 General

Welcome to my personal blog! I'm 万家骏 (Jiajun Wan), an undergraduate student at Guangdong University of Technology, majoring in Electronic Information Engineering.

Why I Started This Blog

I decided to start this blog as a way to document my learning journey, share research insights, and record my thoughts on various topics related to machine learning, AI safety, and theoretical computer science.

Topics I'll Cover

Here are some topics I plan to write about:

  • Research Notes - Notes from my research projects on differential privacy, machine learning theory, and AI safety
  • Paper Reading - Summaries and insights from reading academic papers
  • Thoughts - My reflections on learning methods, research experiences, and career development
  • Life Updates - Occasional updates about my progress and achievements

"The more that you read, the more things you will know. The more that you learn, the more places you'll go." — Dr. Seuss

A Little About My Research

My research interests include:

  • Large Language Models - Research on LLMs
  • Machine Learning - Foundations of ML

I've been fortunate to work with wonderful supervisors including Prof. Zhu Jian, Prof. Zhao Song, and Prof. Qin Jinghui.
